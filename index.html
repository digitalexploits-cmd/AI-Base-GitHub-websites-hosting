Executive Overview

NEXUS is the integrated intelligence layer that transforms legacy electrical infrastructure into a high-resolution sensor network for predictive maintenance. It combines SINE~WaiV signal processing, edge deployment, and structured decision support without requiring additional invasive instrumentation.
