EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW

NEXUS is the integrated intelligence layer that transforms legacy electrical infrastructure into a high-resolution sensor network for predictive maintenance. By combining SINE~WaiV signal processing with multi-agent reasoning, edge deployment, and structured decision support, NEXUS delivers actionable foresight on motor health, load dynamics, and system-level degradation — without requiring additional sensors or invasive instrumentation.

Core Thesis

Recover the high-resolution physical-state information already embedded in existing current and voltage waveforms — turning every motor into a sensor.
