A Milesight CT10x current transformer installed at the MCC bus captured a 120-second full-load acquisition on a 75 HP pump motor with 34,000 hours of runtime in a sugar syrup CIP environment. ESA analysis revealed confirmed broken rotor bar sidebands at both lower and upper positions above the −26 dB action threshold, combined with static air gap eccentricity at 11.2% modulation index, supply voltage unbalance at 2.8% (2.8× the NEMA 1.0% limit), and elevated rotor slot harmonics at the 23rd and 25th orders. Root-cause sequencing determined the voltage unbalance to be the primary accelerant driving rotor bar fatigue — correcting it at the MCC is the first-priority action before any mechanical repair. AI Base³ delivered a prescriptive work order with OEM part numbers, labor estimates, and cost-benefit analysis within the same engagement window, entirely non-invasive, without removing a single panel cover.

Current signature analysis identified anomalous interharmonic content in the 180–240 Hz range consistent with partial discharge activity in stator slot liners. Motor had no prior maintenance record. ESA finding triggered a PI test and Megger confirmation, revealing phase-to-phase insulation resistance at 820 kΩ — below the 1 MΩ action threshold for a motor of this frame at operating temperature. Scheduled rewind was coordinated with a planned production shutdown, eliminating unplanned downtime entirely.
